Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Topsail Solutions sets up operations systems, automations, lead replies, websites and marketing for small businesses. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address, phone number and anything you choose to tell us.
  • When you fill out our setup form: details about your business, such as your services, prices, team members' first names, hours and service area.
  • This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. There are no advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent anyone's information.

Your customers' information

We never ask you to send us your customers' or patients' information by email or through our forms. The systems we set up run in your own Google account, so your schedule and customer details are stored there, under your control, not in an account we own.

While we set up or support your system, we may need to work inside it with your permission. We look only at what's needed for that work and don't copy your customers' information out of your account.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you, prepare your demo and set up your system.
  • To send invoices and updates about work we're doing for you.

We share information only with the services we use to run the business, such as Google Workspace for email and documents, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email bmichaels@topsailsolutions.com and we'll take care of it.
