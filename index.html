Where small businesses lose time and customers.

Most owners don't need more software. They need the follow-up to happen on its own. Topsail sets that up in the Google tools you already have.

  • Leads wait too long

    A new inquiry sits for hours while you're on a job, and they book whoever answered first.

  • The day runs on texts

    Bookings, reminders and who goes where live in your phone and your head, so things slip.

  • Nobody can find you

    Customers search on Google, and a competitor with more reviews and a better website shows up first.

One job, start to finish

Every job moves through the same steps. Topsail handles the follow-up at each one, so you only do the parts that need a person.

  1. New lead

    An inquiry comes in from your website or Google. A reply in your voice is ready within minutes.

  2. Booked

    The job lands on your schedule, and the customer gets a confirmation right away.

  3. Assigned

    You pick who goes. They're notified, and the customer gets a reminder the day before.

  4. Done

    Your team member marks it done. If the job can't happen, a new time gets booked.

  5. Paid

    The invoice goes out, and a polite reminder follows if it isn't paid.

  6. Reviewed

    A couple of hours later, a review request goes out. Unhappy replies come to you, not Google.

Inside an operations system

Bookings in one place
Requests from your website, booking tool or phone calls land on one schedule, with confirmations and reminders sent for you.
Routes on every phone
Each team member gets their stops each morning, with one button to open the whole route in Google Maps.
A live dashboard
See the whole day at a glance: who's where, what's late, and what needs attention before it becomes a problem.
Reschedules handled
Log why a job didn't happen. A new time gets booked, and the customer is told.
Reports for clients and partners
Repeat clients, property managers or partner facilities get a monthly report of the work you did for them.
Owner insights
Revenue, jobs, repeat customers, busiest days and where your customers come from, updated as you work.
