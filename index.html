One visit, start to finish

Every visit moves through the same steps. The system does the follow-up at each one, so your team only does the parts that need a person.

  1. Booked

    The request comes in from your website or booking form. The patient gets a confirmation right away.

  2. Assigned

    You pick who goes. They're notified, and the patient gets a reminder the day before.

  3. On the way

    One tap on your team member's phone sends the patient an "on my way" message.

  4. Collected

    The collection time is logged. If the draw doesn't happen, a new try is booked automatically.

  5. At the lab

    The drop-off and tracking number are recorded, along with when results are due.

  6. Results in

    If results run late, you get a heads-up before the ordering office calls you.
